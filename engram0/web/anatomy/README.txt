# Anatomy in My Little Brain × Engram-0

The brain uses reconstructed public human template geometry:
- Cortical folds: FreeSurfer `fsaverage` pial surfaces at 41k vertices per hemisphere.
- Internal structures: ENIGMA Toolbox subcortical template surfaces (left/right accumbens, amygdala, caudate, hippocampus, pallidum, putamen, thalamus, lateral ventricles).
- Hindbrain & callosum: `aseg.mgz` cerebellar hemispheres, brainstem, ventral diencephalon, cerebral white matter, corpus callosum, and 3rd/4th ventricles.
- Microcircuit coupling: 158 Google H01 C3 segments (`#interneuron #L2 NSe>=800 <NSi`) + IBM-1 macroscopic neural mass coupling.
